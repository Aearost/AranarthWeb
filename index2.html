What You Can Do

A Living, Breathing World

🌊
Elemental Bending

Master Water, Earth, Fire, or Air — and unlock sub-elements through progression. Your bending style defines your role in the world.

⚔
Dominions & Outposts

Claim territory, build outposts, and engage in territorial warfare. Defend your borders with purchasable guards and fortifications.

📜
Rank & Quest System

Progress through Aranarth's ranks via daily and weekly quests. Each rank unlocks new abilities, regions, and privileges.

🏪
Shop Islands & Jobs

Open your own player shop island and take up one of nine Jobs - from Builder to Farmer to Alchemist - to build your wealth.

🐉
Elemental Mounts

Bond with mounts tied to your element. Level them through adventure and unlock unique abilities and bonuses on the battlefield.

🌬
Custom Seasons & Calendar

Aranarth runs on its own calendar - with longer summers, shorter winters, and world events tied to the passage of time.
