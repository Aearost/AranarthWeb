Features

What's on the Server

☯
Bending

Master Water, Earth, Fire, or Air and unlock sub-elements like lightning, metal, lava, blood, and plantbending as you progress. Many abilities are custom-built for Aranarth, and two bending arenas are always open.

✨
The Avatar

One player at a time is chosen as the Avatar, able to bend every element and wield unique abilities like Light of Raava and Past Lives. When the Avatar falls, the cycle moves on.

🛡
Aranarthium

End-game armour forged from a custom ore. Seven sets - Aquatic, Ardent, Dwarven, Elven, Fae, Scorched, and Soulbound - each with their own powers, from double jumps and bonus harvests to strength in the Nether.

🏹
Quivers, Arrows & Potions

Craft eleven custom arrows at the fletching table - explosive, lightning, gust, rooting, and more - and carry them in a quiver that refills your hand as you fire. Potions get their own auto-refilling pouch, plus a book of custom brews to discover.

🔮
Incantations

Bind one incantation to a weapon or tool: Lifesteal, Beheading, Magnetism, Plentiful, Preservation, or Resilience.

⚔
Dominions & Outposts

Claim land with your dominion, expand through outposts, and summon defenders - from creepers to elder guardians - to hold your borders against other players.

🌍
Weather, World Events, & Calendar

Aranarth runs on its own fourteen-month calendar with custom weather. Seasons bring snow and thaw, and rare world events like Solaris, Lunaris, and Seiko's Comet change how the world plays.

💰
Economy & Jobs

Run a shop, take on one of eight Jobs, and trade on a market whose prices move with supply and demand.

📜
Ranks & Quests

Climb the ranks through daily and weekly quests, keep up a login streak, and open crates for rare rewards. Higher ranks unlock new features across the server.

🌱
Quality of Life

Auto-compressing items, pickup blacklists, homepads for fast travel, and a reaper who'll sell back your last death's inventory - small touches that make survival smoother.

Join the Discord

The Discord is where most server activity happens outside of the game - announcements, updates, support, and general chat.
